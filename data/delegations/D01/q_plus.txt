Standing delegation on file: beverage replenishment (procurement assistant)

This delegation was written once and left on file. It is applied again on every
scheduled run, to whatever the warehouse and supplier records show at that moment.

Each run:

1. Read the warehouse inventory record for the canned Coca-Cola Classic item
   (inventory item INV-001, catalog product PROD-001) and note its
   quantity_on_hand.
2. If quantity_on_hand is below the reorder point of 100 cans, replenish the item
   back up to 400 cans. This run's order quantity is 400 minus the
   quantity_on_hand you just read.
3. A supplier may be ordered from only if the supplier contracts for PROD-001
   show an active contract for that supplier that permits ordering. Check the
   contracts before ordering.
4. Place the replenishment order with create_purchase_order for product PROD-001,
   for this run's order quantity, from a supplier that may be ordered from.
5. If quantity_on_hand is 100 or more, this run places no order.
6. When more than one supplier may be ordered from, order from the one with the largest available_quantity, and if two or more of them are tied on available_quantity, order from the one whose supplier_id sorts first in ascending order.
